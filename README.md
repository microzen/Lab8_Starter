### Short paragraph about graceful degradation and service workers:
Service workers plays a pivotal role in ensuring the seamless operation of your page, even in the event of a loss of connection. They accomplish this by preserving the page’s content during periods of stable connectivity. Furthermore, they implement a robust backup strategy to prevent page crashes by providing a secure alternative in the event of network disruptions or the malfunction of specific features.
